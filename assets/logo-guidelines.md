Logo usage guidelines for Goiye's IT Services

Files added:
- assets/logo.svg (stacked mark + wordmark)
- assets/logo-horizontal.svg (horizontal wordmark)
- assets/logo-mark.svg (icon-only mark)

Colors:
- Primary gradient: #06b6d4 → #0ea5a4
- Dark text: #0f172a
- Muted text: #64748b

Typography:
- Use a clean sans-serif: Segoe UI, Roboto, system-ui, Arial as fallback.

Usage:
- Use logo-mark.svg for favicons and small badges.
- Use logo-horizontal.svg for headers and navbars.
- Use logo.svg for hero sections and marketing where vertical space is available.
